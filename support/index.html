Ordo shows what the Catholic Church celebrates each day, the feast, the Sunday or season with its Gospel, or the saint, with a great painting, following the U.S. liturgical calendar.

Putting the day's painting on your Lock Screen

iPhone apps can't change your wallpaper by themselves, so Ordo does it through Apple's Shortcuts app. Set it up once:

  1. Open Ordo, scroll to the bottom of any day and tap Set Up Lock Screen.
  2. Tap Add Shortcut, then Add Shortcut again in Shortcuts.
  3. Back in Ordo, tap Try It Now. Today's painting appears on your Lock Screen.
  4. Tap Open the Shortcut, then Automation is turned off, then Run Immediately. From now on the picture changes by itself just after midnight.

If the picture looks shifted or blurry

Your Lock Screen probably has the 3D effect on. Press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then tap the 3D button (the hexagon, bottom right) to turn it off. In the ⋯ menu, turn off Perspective Zoom too.

Widgets

Press and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit (or Customize), then Add Widget, and choose Ordo. The widgets change by themselves each day.
