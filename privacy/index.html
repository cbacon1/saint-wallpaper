Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

Ordo collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

This policy covers the Ordo app for iPhone (with its widgets and its “Get Today's Ordo Wallpaper” action for the Shortcuts app), the Ordo app for Mac, and this website.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, and the paintings and texts the app has already downloaded, are kept only on your iPhone or Mac. They are not sent anywhere.
  • The Lock Screen picture is drawn on your iPhone. The Shortcuts app sets it as your wallpaper; Ordo never sees your photos or your other wallpapers.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What the app downloads

To show each day's feast, saint or Gospel, the app downloads the calendar, the texts and the paintings from this website. It sends no information about you to do so: the same files go to everyone. The Mac app also checks this website for updates to itself.

This website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Like any web server, GitHub's servers receive the technical details that come with every web request, such as your device's internet address, and may keep them in their logs for security. GitHub handles that information under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive it and don't use it to identify anyone.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. The home page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google when you visit it; see Google's privacy policy. This privacy page does not.

Children

Ordo collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
