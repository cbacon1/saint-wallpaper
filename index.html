Saint Wallpaper

Every morning your Mac's desktop becomes the saint or feast of the day — a great painting of them, shown whole, with a short life of the saint and a note on the picture. On plain weekdays and Sundays it shows a painting of that day's Gospel.

Download for Mac

Free · macOS 14 Sonoma or newer · follows the U.S. Catholic calendar (worldwide calendar in Settings)

Installing

  1. Open the downloaded file. It unzips into Saint Wallpaper.
  2. Drag Saint Wallpaper into your Applications folder, then double-click it.
  3. The first time, your Mac will say it can't check the app for malicious software, because it isn't sold through Apple. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to "Saint Wallpaper". You only do this once.
  4. A small ✝ appears at the top of your screen and your wallpaper changes. That's it — it updates by itself every day and starts when you log in.

The paintings and texts live on this website, so corrections and new pictures reach everyone automatically. The app needs the internet now and then to fetch the next two weeks of pictures; it works offline in between.

About the pictures

The paintings, icons, mosaics and engravings are works in the public domain or under free licenses, from Wikimedia Commons. Each wallpaper names the artist, and the ✝ menu has the full list of credits.
