Ordo Get it for Mac

This is on the desktop and Lock Screen of everyone who has Ordo today. Tomorrow it changes by itself.

The rest of the week

    On a Mac

    1. Download the app and open the file. It unzips into Ordo.
    2. Drag Ordo into your Applications folder and double-click it.
    3. Your Mac will say it can't check the app, because it isn't sold through Apple. Click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
    4. The ✝ appears at the top of your screen and your wallpaper changes. It starts by itself when you log in.

    On an iPhone

    Get Ordo on the App Store. It has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, every day's painting and story, and can put a new picture on your Lock Screen every day.

    The paintings, icons, mosaics and engravings are in the public domain or freely licensed, from Wikimedia Commons. Every wallpaper names its artist, and the ✝ menu lists full credits.
